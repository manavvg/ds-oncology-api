Who we are

About D&S
Oncopharma

Founded with a singular focus on oncology, we bridge the gap between top-tier API manufacturers and pharmaceutical companies that demand precision, reliability, and full regulatory support at every stage.

Our mission

Enabling Better Cancer Treatments Through Reliable API Supply

D&S Oncopharma was established to address a critical need in the pharmaceutical supply chain: a specialist partner who deeply understands the unique complexities of oncology API procurement.

Cancer medicines demand the highest standards. From cold-chain handling and stability requirements to complex regulatory filings and DMF submissions, every step requires specialist knowledge that generalist distributors simply cannot offer.

Our network spans GMP-certified manufacturers across Asia, Europe, and the Americas giving clients access to competitive pricing, multiple sourcing options, and supply continuity even in disrupted global markets.

We work with generic pharmaceutical manufacturers, formulation companies, research institutions, and contract development organisations who require dependable API supply with complete documentation packages.

Specialist Focus

Our entire portfolio is dedicated to oncology. This means deeper product knowledge, stronger manufacturer relationships, and faster more accurate responses to your enquiries.

Documentation Excellence

Every API we supply comes with a comprehensive documentation package: CoA, MSDS, DMF cross-reference, stability data where available, and regulatory letter of access support.

Global Reach

We supply manufacturers in 40+ countries across South Asia, Southeast Asia, Africa, Latin America, and Eastern Europe with logistics partners specialised in pharmaceutical freight.

Quality Assurance

All APIs are tested against pharmacopoeia specifications. Batch-specific CoA and third-party testing reports are available on request for every shipment.
