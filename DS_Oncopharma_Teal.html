Who we are

About D&S Oncopharma

Founded with a singular focus on oncology, we bridge the gap between top-tier API manufacturers and pharmaceutical companies that demand precision, reliability, and full regulatory support.

Our mission

Enabling Better Cancer Treatments Through Reliable API Supply

D&S Oncopharma was established to address a critical need in the pharmaceutical supply chain: a specialist partner who understands the unique complexities of oncology API procurement.

Cancer medicines demand the highest standards. From cold-chain handling to complex regulatory filings, every step of the supply process requires specialist knowledge. We have built our entire operation around these demands.

Our network spans GMP-certified manufacturers across Asia, Europe, and the Americas — giving our clients access to competitive pricing, diverse sourcing options, and supply continuity even in disrupted markets.

We work with generic pharmaceutical manufacturers, formulation companies, research institutions, and contract manufacturers who require dependable API supply with complete documentation packages.

Specialist Focus

Unlike broad-spectrum API distributors, our entire portfolio is dedicated to oncology. This specialisation means deeper product knowledge, better manufacturer relationships, and faster response to your enquiries.

Documentation Excellence

Every API we supply comes with a comprehensive documentation package: Certificate of Analysis, MSDS, DMF cross-reference, stability data where available, and regulatory letter of access support.

Global Reach

We supply pharmaceutical manufacturers in over 40 countries across South Asia, Southeast Asia, Africa, Latin America, and Eastern Europe — with logistics partners specialised in pharmaceutical freight.

Quality Assurance

All sourced APIs are tested against pharmacopoeia specifications. Batch-specific CoA and third-party testing reports are available on request for every shipment.
